## Reason H8 is not performed

This is a single-author, unfunded study and the independent qualified clinician raters and associated governance/logistical resources required by the registered H8 design are not available.

The decision was made before any human ratings existed, so it is not outcome- or performance-driven.

## Manuscript consequence

No H8 agreement or construct-validity result exists.

The manuscript must:

- state that clinician construct validation was not performed;
- avoid claiming clinician-validated interpretability of the eight scores;
- list human construct validation as future work/limitation.

The frozen sample and packet hashes remain provenance only. The manual H8 evaluator should not be submitted for Paper 1.
