## Figure 1. Preregistered design and registered analyses show no consistent incremental discrimination from low-dimensional semantic scores

**Legend.** **a,** Corrected v2.1 study design. Adult MIMIC-III MetaVision ICU stays were evaluated at a fixed 12-hour landmark. The narrative input was the single latest eligible bedside note before the landmark; the primary corpus removed prespecified direct endpoint or treatment expressions before semantic inference. Eight typed semantic scores were added to a rich structured comparator containing 34 physiology/laboratory/urine features, treatment/support context, documentation-behavior variables, and ordinary note context. Outcomes were invasive ventilation, renal replacement therapy (RRT), and ICU death during the subsequent 12 hours. CareVue ICU death was analyzed separately as a replication. **b,** Delta area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUROC), defined as augmented model minus its corresponding comparator, for the registered primary Open-Jev analyses, alternative semantic instruments, endpoint and prospective-timing sensitivities, the separate CareVue ICU-death replication, and registered patient-shuffled semantic negative controls. Points are primary-partition estimates and horizontal lines are 95% patient-cluster refit-bootstrap percentile intervals. Marker shape distinguishes primary, sensitivity/timing, replication, and shuffled-control analyses. The pale band shown only on each primary row spans the approximate pre-analysis 80% detectable full-cohort |delta AUROC| (0.0233 for ventilation, 0.0113 for RRT, and 0.0241 for MetaVision ICU death). These planning values are shown only as precision context and are not equivalence margins or decision thresholds. MetaVision and CareVue ICU-death analyses were not pooled.

**Alt text:** Two-panel scientific figure. Panel a is a left-to-right schematic of the preregistered MIMIC-III design: adult MetaVision ICU stays, a 12-hour landmark, the single latest eligible pre-landmark bedside note, eight typed semantic scores, comparison of a rich structured model with and without the semantic scores, and prediction of ventilation, renal replacement therapy, or ICU death during the next 12 hours. Panel b is a comprehensive forest plot grouped by outcome. Primary, sensitivity, timing, replication, and patient-shuffled-control delta-AUROC estimates cluster around zero, and every displayed 95% interval spans zero. Narrow pale bands on the three primary rows show the approximate pre-analysis detectable magnitudes for context only.

## Figure 2. Representation comparison and exploratory analyses clarify the near-null primary result

**Legend.** **a,** Post-registration exploratory decomposition of Open-Jev incremental AUROC across four nested HistGradientBoosting comparators: A, 34 structured physiology/laboratory/urine features; B, A plus treatment/support context and death-specific code status; C, B plus documentation-behavior variables; and D, C plus ordinary note context (note availability, selected-note age, and note category), equal to the registered rich comparator. The filled blue line shows the prespecified primary frozen partition and pale open lines show four additional frozen partitions. No new bootstrap was performed. For ventilation, levels A and B require the note-availability caveat because semantic-score missingness can reveal note availability before documentation/note-context variables enter the comparator. **b,** Registered secondary representation comparison within the same L2-penalized logistic-regression family. Points show primary-partition delta AUROC for comparator plus Open-Jev, comparator plus TF-IDF (up to 10,000 lexical features), and the Open-Jev increment after TF-IDF. No refit-bootstrap interval was prespecified for this comparison; all five frozen partitions are reported in Supplementary Table S3. **c,** Post-registration label-free alignment audit. Filled blue points show Spearman correlations between hemodynamic or respiratory concern scores and clinically corresponding pre-landmark support states; open points show the same correlations after complete semantic-score vectors were shuffled between different patients. Outcome labels were not read for this audit. **d,** Post-registration exploratory note-available subgroup. Points show delta AUROC from the already-frozen full-cohort out-of-fold predictions after restriction to rows with an eligible note. The filled blue point is the primary frozen partition and open points are the four additional frozen partitions. Models were not refit in the subgroup.

**Alt text:** Four-panel scientific figure explaining the near-null primary result. Panel a shows Open-Jev incremental AUROC across progressively richer structured comparators. Ventilation has a positive increment over physiology alone and after treatment/support context, but the increment becomes mixed around zero after documentation behavior and note context are added; RRT and ICU-death increments remain near zero. Panel b compares representations within the same logistic model family: TF-IDF has a small positive increment for all three outcomes, while Open-Jev and Open-Jev after TF-IDF are negative. Panel c pairs real and shuffled construct-state Spearman correlations; matched hemodynamic and respiratory scores consistently correlate more strongly with corresponding support states than shuffled references. Panel d shows five frozen-partition delta-AUROC estimates among note-available patients; their directions are mixed and no outcome shows a stable positive increment.
